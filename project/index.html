How Nice You Are
(Walankin raki wa7d lmtnga mnk makayn)

❤️

I didn’t meet you in some planned or expected way. It was at EST Khenifra that everything started. From the very first conversations, we weren’t talking about boring things—we were already joking around, talking about our shared frustrations, even roasting the teachers who drove us crazy. And in that chaos, I got to know someone I never expected to find "you".

To others, you might seem cold, serious, always wrapped in your coat and hijab, hiding behind those glasses. Someone who takes ages to get ready and barely cracks a smile. But to me? You’re the silly, clumsy, hilarious mess of a person who somehow brings laughter into everything. You're the one who always has a huge problem every-day usually with a solution sitting right in front of you (which makes me wonder why you even wear those glasses). And honestly? That’s part of your charm.

You’ve helped me without realizing it. You've listened, cared, and stayed. And no matter how chaotic things get, you’re still one of the sweetest souls I’ve ever met.

Why You Are a Good Friend (but a bad cook)

⭐

I’m not going back on my word you’re a bad cook. Even when you were willing to prepare harsha, you somehow ended up creating a mix of batbot, harsha, and a hint of keika, all in one unholy creation. It wasn’t food it was Frankenstein on a plate. Honestly, I’ve never seen something so confused about its identity.

And don’t try to argue deep down, you know your cooking capabilities. The funniest part? I’m a guy and I still cook better than you. But hey, we survived, right?

Now, if I had to describe why you're a good friend, this website would need infinite gigabytes just to begin. But if I had to sum it up in one sentence: it’s because you’re always there.

Through the tough days, the weird ones, the breakdowns and overthinking you’re present. You share everything with me, literally everything. Even your fish-shaped plates somehow remind you of me (don’t ask me why, I still don’t get it).

You’re the type of friend who brings chaos, laughter, and support all wrapped in one silly, lovable human.

I wish you a life filled with joy, crazy laughter, and small moments that make big memories. I hope you keep being your weird, silly self, and that life gives you exactly what your heart asks for even if it's as confusing and mixed up as your Harsha-Batbot-Keika combo.

I wish you a life of love, confidence, and calm and maybe one day, who knows… to be a happy wife (yes, Wld Sale, you know the deal).

You’re one of a kind and don’t let anyone, not even yourself, forget it.
